## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, contemplative, and intellectually rigorous atmosphere reminiscent of a private literary library or an independent architectural reading room. It deliberately rejects the sterile, high-gloss transactional paradigms of modern e-commerce and cold SaaS metrics. The experience prioritizes humanistic discovery, deep narrative browsing, and serene focus.

The visual style is **Soft Editorial Neumorphism** balanced with **Restrained Glassmorphism**. Physical tactile cues are articulated through subtle porcelain-raised cards, sculpted bevels, and warm stone recessed wells, avoiding both aggressive skeuomorphism and flat, uninspired cards. Glassmorphism is strictly rationed for elevated floating capsules—primarily the primary navigation bar and active search filter pills—preserving visual hierarchy and maintaining spatial lightness.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in tactile organic materials: Warm Ivory paper, Soft Porcelain vellum, Warm Stone limestone, and deep Ink Green printing pigment. It operates strictly in light mode.

- **Primary (`#185C58` — Deep Teal):** Directs the visual anchor. Used strictly for high-priority interactive calls to action, active navigation indicators, and focused inputs.
- **Secondary (`#A8BDAE` — Soft Sage):** Supports passive category identifiers, secondary badges, and subtle borders. Never used for small body copy or low-contrast critical metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#C58B70` — Muted Terracotta):** Reserved for curated editorial highlights, staff picks, active reading tags, and subtle visual punctuation.
- **Neutral (`#263633` — Ink Green):** Deep pigment replacing generic carbon black. Serves as the primary typographic color across all reading surfaces, providing exceptional contrast without harsh optical vibration.
- **Background & Surfaces (`#F5F1E9` and `#FCFAF5`):** The foundational canvas is Warm Ivory, while elevated reading cards and panels leverage Soft Porcelain to produce organic, readable physical depth.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs a literary transitional serif with a clean, highly legible neo-grotesque sans-serif.

- **Lora (Headlines & Editorial Display):** Injected with humanistic book-print qualities. It drives all high-level narrative introductions, book titles, and editorial curators' notes. Letter-spacing is slightly condensed in large display sizes to replicate traditional hot-metal publishing typesetting.
- **Inter (Interface, Metadata, & System Controls):** Handles functional legibility across varying viewports. Tabular figures and balanced medium weights ensure structured book meta, author indices, catalog filters, and navigation anchors remain crisp and unencumbered.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum canvas container of `1360px` to maintain optimal book jacket proportions and typographic measure.

- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns with a `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutter and `3rem` (`48px`) outer margin. Book grids comfortably deploy across 4 or 5 columns without compressing jacket aspect ratios below visual balance.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns with a `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutter and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Book collections flex to 3 columns; discovery sidebars collapse into sticky drawer filters.
- **Mobile (390px – 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins. Book grids adapt to 2 columns with fixed 2:3 aspect ratio cover constraints, or transition into touch-momentum horizontal carousels with peek margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages soft physical layering, avoiding harsh digital drop shadows:

1. **Recessed & Inset (Wells & Form Controls):** Search bars, filter wells, and inactive input inputs sit physically recessed beneath the page plane using dual interior shadows:
   - `inset 2px 2px 5px rgba(38, 54, 51, 0.08), inset -2px -2px 5px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`
2. **Resting Porcelain Cards (Soft Surface):** Default book cards, genre clusters, and panels sit gently lifted above the Warm Ivory canvas:
   - `0 4px 12px rgba(38, 54, 51, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(38, 54, 51, 0.04)`
3. **Elevated & Hovering:** Interactive targets lift upon hover with expanded diffusion:
   - `0 10px 24px rgba(38, 54, 51, 0.09), 0 3px 8px rgba(38, 54, 51, 0.04)`
4. **Selective Glassmorphism (Floating Shell):** Floating navigation capsules, live search overlays, and transient dialogs feature semi-opaque Soft Porcelain surfaces (`rgba(252, 250, 245, 0.82)`), backed by a `16px` backdrop-filter blur, anchored with a hairline `1px` translucent border (`rgba(234, 227, 216, 0.65)`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances tailored architectural radii with organic pill capsules:

- **Pill Capsules (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic navigational floating headers, search bars, contextual category filter chips, and primary CTA buttons.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`16px` / `24px`):** Literary collection cards, book detail containers, and genre modules leverage refined, organic corners matching token `roundedness: 2` (base `8px` up to `16px` on book card structures, and `24px` for large editorial panels).
- **Controls & Wells (`8px` / `12px`):** Inset input boxes, catalog dropdowns, and rating modules use structured `12px` bounds to ensure internal text remains grounded.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Pill capsule (`9999px`), Deep Teal background (`#185C58`), white text (`#FCFAF5`), label-md Inter typography. Hover applies subtle scale (1.02) and `--shadow-raised`. Active state depresses with `--shadow-inset`.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill capsule, Soft Porcelain background, hairline border in Warm Stone (`#EAE3D8`), Ink Green text.
- **Editorial Text Links:** Ink Green text with a permanent or animated underline rendered in Muted Terracotta (`#C58B70`).

### Search Inputs & Recessed Wells
- Pill or `12px` rounded container with Warm Stone fill (`#EAE3D8`) and the inset dual shadow system. Ink Green placeholder with warm grey secondary tone (`#827E75`). When active/focused, the container transitions to Soft Porcelain with a `2px` focus stroke of Deep Teal and no harsh outline.

### Book Cards & Collections
- **Standard Discovery Card:** Soft Porcelain background, `16px` radius, subtle soft shadow. Book jacket image adheres strictly to a 2:3 vertical aspect ratio, recessed slightly within a `2px` border to emulate real physical binding. 
- **Typography Stacking:** Book title in Lora bold (`headline-sm`), author in Inter (`body-sm`), followed by category tags styled in Soft Sage pill containers.
- **Curated Collections:** Wide editorial cards (`24px` radius) featuring asymmetric offset book cover arrangements, paired with italicized curator pull-quotes.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Pill radius (`9999px`), 32px height, Inter `label-sm`. Default state sits flush with a hairline border. Selected state transitions to solid Deep Teal background with crisp white typography. Hover state incorporates a subtle tinted sage background fill (`rgba(168, 189, 174, 0.2)`).

### Navigation Capsule & Overlays
- Floating island container centered at the top of the viewport. Translucent Porcelain surface (`rgba(252, 250, 245, 0.85)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `1px` translucent border, floating shadow elevation. Active links marked with Deep Teal indicators.
